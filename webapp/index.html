Backtrail

About

Backtrail is an independent off-grid messaging app for goTenna Mesh® radios and Meshtastic® LoRa radios. It connects to radios you own over Bluetooth (or USB for Meshtastic in desktop browsers) to send and receive text messages, check-ins, locations, and SOS alerts; keep a message history and contacts; show people and your breadcrumb trail on a map; list the nodes on a Meshtastic mesh; and read and change supported radio settings. It needs no account, no servers, and no internet connection.

Message Privacy

Radio transmissions can be received by any compatible device in range. On goTenna, messages sent without encryption are public. When you set a group passphrase and turn on encryption, the message content (including any location) and your nickname are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from that passphrase, so only devices running Backtrail with the same passphrase can read them. On Meshtastic, messages use the encryption of the channel they are sent on: the default public channel's key is widely known, so treat it as public, and anyone with a channel's link or QR code can read that channel. Encryption does not hide that a message was sent, its size, its timing, or the sender's device ID, and anyone who knows a passphrase or channel key can read every message protected by it. Locations you send in a check-in, location share, or SOS are visible to everyone who can read that message. Share passphrases, invite codes, and channel QR codes only with people you trust. No encryption is guaranteed to be unbreakable; do not rely on this app to protect information where disclosure could cause serious harm.

Privacy

No account is required and there is no sign-in. BlackFox Media LLC operates no server for this app and receives no data from it. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party trackers. No personal data is collected, sold, or shared by BlackFox Media LLC.

Stored on your device only: your nickname, IDs, passphrase, settings, message history, contacts, and breadcrumb trail. You can delete them in Settings → Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Location: used only while the app is open, when you open the map, record a trail, or send a location, check-in, or SOS. Your location leaves your device only inside messages you choose to send over radio. Backtrail never tracks your location in the background.

Camera: used only to scan QR codes when you tap Scan QR. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications: shown on your device for incoming messages; they are not sent through any server.

Optional street map: off by default. If you turn it on, map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which receive your device's IP address and the map area you view, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy. The default radar map uses no internet.
