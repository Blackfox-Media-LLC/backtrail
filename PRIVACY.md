# Backtrail privacy

The published policy is the Backtrail section of https://www.blackfox-media.com/privacy-policy
(source: `App Releases/Backtrail/store-listing/privacy-policy-backtrail.html`; the in-app About screen
carries the same summary).

In short:
- No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. BlackFox Media LLC receives nothing.
- Stored only on the device: nickname, IDs, passphrase, settings, message history, contacts, breadcrumb trail.
- Leaves the device only by the user's action, over radio to other radios: messages, and the user's location
  when they send a location, SOS, or check-in with location turned on.
- Location is foreground-only; camera is used only to scan QR codes; notifications are local.
- Optional street map (off by default) downloads OpenStreetMap tiles, which reveals the viewed area and IP address
  to OpenStreetMap's servers.
